Invigilation Control Center

Exam Invigilation Management

Auto-assigns faculty room-wise using your policy: capacity <= 10 gets 1 invigilator, capacity > 10 gets 2. Assignments are persistent and fully editable.

Conflict Guardrails

No faculty can be assigned to multiple rooms for the same exam/date/shift. Existing same-slot assignments in other exams are also blocked.

Examination Duties

Each teacher can be assigned one of the following roles:

  • Hall Invigilator
  • Relief Invigilator (Reliever)
  • Flying Squad
  • Chief Superintendent
  • Deputy Superintendent
  • Control Room Staff
  • Corridor Supervisor
  • Reserve Teacher
  • Attendance Verification Officer
  • Question Paper Distribution Staff
  • Answer Sheet Collection Staff
